<h1 align="center">Borealis</h1>

<p align="center">
  A lightweight, multi-account WhatsApp desktop client.
</p>

Borealis keeps all of your WhatsApp accounts in one window, so you can switch between personal and work chats in a click. Each account stays signed in on its own, and Borealis stays light on your computer.

## Features

- **All your accounts in one place.** Add as many WhatsApp accounts as you need. They stay completely separate from each other.
- **Unread counts at a glance.** Every account shows how many chats are waiting for you.
- **Search everything.** Find people, messages and files across all of your accounts at once.
- **Never miss a message.** Get notifications from every account, even the ones you're not looking at.
- **Voice and video calls.** Call right from the app on macOS and Windows, just like on WhatsApp Web.
- **Your layout.** Keep your accounts in a sidebar, or in a slim bar along the top.
- **You're in control.** Decide whether WhatsApp may use notifications, your microphone and your camera, or ask every time.

## Installation

Download Borealis for your computer from [getborealis.app](https://getborealis.app) or the [latest release](../../releases/latest).

| System  | Download       | How to install                                                                               |
| ------- | -------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| macOS   | `.dmg`         | Open it and drag Borealis into your Applications folder. Needs macOS 14 or later.            |
| Windows | `-setup.exe`   | Run the installer. If Windows warns you about it, choose **More info**, then **Run anyway**. |
| Linux   | `.AppImage`    | Make it executable (`chmod +x Borealis*.AppImage`), then open it.                            |
| Arch    | `.pkg.tar.zst` | Install it with `sudo pacman -U borealis-*.pkg.tar.zst`.                                     |

## Keyboard shortcuts

| Action                   | macOS                                     | Windows and Linux                             |
| ------------------------ | ----------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------- |
| Switch to account 1 to 9 | <kbd>⌘</kbd> <kbd>1</kbd> to <kbd>9</kbd> | <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> <kbd>1</kbd> to <kbd>9</kbd>  |
| Search                   | <kbd>⌘</kbd> <kbd>F</kbd>                 | <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> <kbd>F</kbd>                  |
| Open settings            | <kbd>⌘</kbd> <kbd>,</kbd>                 | <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> <kbd>,</kbd>                  |
| Show or hide the sidebar | <kbd>⇧</kbd> <kbd>⌘</kbd> <kbd>S</kbd>    | <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> <kbd>Shift</kbd> <kbd>S</kbd> |
| Quit                     | <kbd>⌘</kbd> <kbd>Q</kbd>                 | <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> <kbd>Q</kbd>                  |

WhatsApp's own shortcuts keep working too, like <kbd>⇧</kbd> <kbd>⌘</kbd> <kbd>F</kbd> to search inside a chat.

## Privacy

Borealis opens WhatsApp Web directly, the same way your browser would. Your messages go straight between you and WhatsApp, and Borealis doesn't collect anything.

Borealis is an independent project and isn't affiliated with WhatsApp or Meta.
